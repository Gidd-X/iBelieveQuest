# **App Name**: iBelieveQuest

## Core Features:

- Article Posting: Allows administrators to create and publish articles related to faith, questions, and spirituality.
- User Comments: Enables users to comment on articles, fostering discussion and community engagement.
- Tagging System: Categorizes articles by topics like 'faith,' 'doubt,' 'Christianity,' etc., for easy navigation.
- AI-Powered Content Suggestion: Based on the content of the article or comment a user is writing, this tool suggests related passages from religious texts, expanding on themes and adding depth.
- Search Functionality: Allows users to search for specific articles or topics of interest.
- Subscription Feature: Allows users to subscribe to receive email notifications for new posts and updates.
- About Us Section: Details the blog's mission and the beliefs and values that guide its content.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep Blue (#3F51B5) to evoke a sense of trust and reflection.
- Background color: Light Gray (#F0F2F5) to provide a clean and calm reading environment.
- Accent color: Gold (#FFC107) for highlighting important elements and CTAs.
- Body font: 'Literata', serif, for comfortable readability and a literary feel.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', serif, for elegant and eye-catching titles.
- Use simple, clean icons related to spirituality and discovery, ensuring they are consistent with the overall design.
- Implement a clear, organized layout that prioritizes readability, with intuitive navigation and well-defined sections.
